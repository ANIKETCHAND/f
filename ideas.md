# Forever Us — Design Brief

## Direction
**Sunlit scrapbook / editorial keepsake** — a warm, slightly grown-up digital scrapbook that feels collected over time: paper-like surfaces, instant-photo frames, hand-drawn marks, and calm modern typography.

## Design movement
Modern editorial scrapbook: clean hierarchy and generous whitespace, softened with tactile details and memory-keeping motifs.

## Core principles
- Memories first: the visual system should make photos, dates, and captions feel precious.
- Playful, not childish: use gestures, stickers, and little surprises without turning the page into a toy.
- Personalizable: labels, names, dates, and media should be easy to swap.
- Warm clarity: high contrast, short copy, obvious navigation, and mobile-friendly tap targets.

## Color philosophy
Use an ink-navy base for legibility, warm cream paper as the canvas, mango yellow for optimism, coral for emotional emphasis, and sage for calm moments. Keep accents flat and lightly imperfect rather than glossy.

## Layout paradigm
A narrow editorial frame with a sticky top navigation and a split hero: left-aligned story copy, right-aligned “memory stack” of instant-photo cards. Follow with a compact proof strip, then alternating timeline and gallery blocks. Cards use staggered offsets and small labels to evoke a box of keepsakes.

## Signature elements
- A simple two-loop “friendship knot” symbol for the logo.
- Tiny date stamps, paper tabs, and hand-drawn underline strokes.
- A “memory stack” hero card with a polaroid silhouette.
- Soft rounded chips for people, places, and mood.
- The game card styled like a prompt card pulled from a jar.

## Interaction philosophy
Interactions should feel like revealing a memory: smooth anchor navigation, hover lift on cards, toast confirmation after adding a memory, and the game prompt flipping/revealing with a small celebratory score update. Everything works without an account.

## Animation
Keep motion short and tactile: 250–450ms ease-out transitions, subtle card lift, a gentle reveal for the game prompt, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography system
Use a confident sans for UI and body (`DM Sans`, with system fallback) paired with a slightly characterful display serif (`Fraunces`, with Georgia fallback) for section titles and emotional copy. Use uppercase mono-like metadata sparingly for dates and labels.

## Brand essence
A living archive of the people who make ordinary days feel like a story.

## Brand voice
Affectionate, specific, gently funny, and conversational. Prefer “the night we…” over generic “create memories.”

## Wordmark / logo
“Forever Us” wordmark with a small two-loop knot / linked-heart mark. The mark should remain recognizable at favicon size and in monochrome.

## Signature brand color
Mango `#F5C451`, grounded by ink `#17202B` and cream `#F8F5EE`.

## Planned visual assets

- `public/forever-us-hero.png`: warm editorial still-life of instant photos, ticket stubs, and handwritten notes on a cream table, no readable text, intended as the hero memory-stack backdrop.
